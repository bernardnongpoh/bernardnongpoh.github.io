Tue 18 Aug (Tue-4) · Topic 1
Slides
L10 — Software specifications (PDF)
Follow

Paper

L11. Hoare logic

Wed 19 Aug (Wed-4) · Topic 1
Slides
L11 — Hoare logic (PDF)
Follow
Svendsen, Hoare Logic and Model Checking (Cambridge), lectures 1–3
Paper
Hoare, “An Axiomatic Basis for Computer Programming”, CACM 12(10), 1969.
Outline
Program state and assertions; the Hoare triple {P} C {Q}; partial vs total correctness; inference rules for skip, assignment, sequence, conditional and consequence; the assignment rule read backwards; the while rule, loop invariants and the three proof obligations.

L12. Invariants & specification inference

Thu 20 Aug (Thu-4) · Topic 1
Slides
L12 — Invariants and specification inference (PDF)
Follow
Naik, “Software Specifications”, CIS 5470 (Penn); JML //@invariant / //@requires annotations
Paper
Flanagan, Leino, “Houdini, an Annotation Assistant for ESC/Java”, FME 2001; Ernst et al., “Dynamically Discovering Likely Program Invariants” (Daikon), TSE 2001.
Outline
Program, loop and class invariants; why validity is not enough — the useful invariant is the one implying the post-condition; inferring specifications instead of writing them; the Houdini algorithm (candidate invariants, refutation, workflow) on buggy and corrected programs; pros and cons.

L13. Coverage & mutation testing

Tue 25 Aug (Tue-5) · Topic 1 → 2
Slides
L13 — Coverage & mutation testing (PDF)
Follow
Zhu, Hall, May, “Software Unit Test Coverage and Adequacy”, ACM CSUR 1997; Aho §9 (CFG background for branch coverage)
Paper
Jia, Harman, “An Analysis and Survey of the Development of Mutation Testing”, TSE 2011.
Outline
Test adequacy — when is a test suite “enough”; statement / line, branch, condition and MC-DC coverage; coverage on the CFG and what coverage does not tell you; instrumenting a program to measure it; mutation testing — mutation operators, mutants and the mutation score; killed vs surviving mutants; equivalent mutants and undecidability; cost and mutant-reduction techniques.

L14. Introduction to fuzzing

Thu 27 Aug (Thu-5) · Topic 2
Slides
L14 — Introduction to fuzzing (PDF)
Follow
FB “Introduction to Fuzzing” & “Greybox Fuzzing”; AFL technical whitepaper (M. Zalewski)
Paper
Miller, Fredriksen, So, “An Empirical Study of the Reliability of UNIX Utilities”, CACM 1990; Böhme, Pham, Roychoudhury, “Coverage-based Greybox Fuzzing as Markov Chain” (AFLFast), CCS 2016; Fioraldi et al., “AFL++”, WOOT 2020.
Outline
Random testing and the 1990 UNIX utilities study; blind vs mutation-based fuzzing; seeds, corpora and mutation operators; what counts as a bug — crashes, assertions and sanitizers (ASan / UBSan / MSan) as oracles; black-, white- and greybox fuzzing; coverage-guided greybox fuzzing (AFL, libFuzzer, AFL++); edge-coverage instrumentation and the coverage bitmap; corpus scheduling and power schedules.
